# 🐱 Pluffy Bird: Flappy Cat Edition 🪽

An arcade Flappy Bird game where you take flight as **Pluffy**—an adorable, chubby kitty who sprouted angel wings and loves gliding through the clouds!

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Double-click `launch_game.bat` (or open [`index.html`](./index.html) directly in any browser like Chrome or Edge).
2. Press **SPACEBAR**, **Up Arrow**, **Left Click**, or **Tap** to flap your wings and meow upwards!
3. Navigate between the giant cat scratching posts and snag **Golden Fish Cookies** (`+2` pts) and **Catnip Stars** (`+5` pts) for bonus score.
4. Don't hit the scratching posts or touch the ground!

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse / Touch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flap / Jump** | `SPACEBAR` or `Up Arrow (⬆)` | Left Click / Tap on screen |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` or `ESC` | Tap Pause button (`⏸️`) |
| **Mute / Unmute** | `M` | Tap Mute button (`🔊`) |
| **Restart** | `SPACEBAR` | Click "Play Again" button |

## ✨ Features

- **🐾 Adorable Chubby Cat**:
  - Procedurally rendered vector cat with fluttery wings, fluffy cheeks, animated ears, swishing tail, and cute toe beans.
  - Expressive reactions: happy `^ ^` eyes when flapping, wide alert eyes when diving fast, and comical `x x` dizzy eyes on collision.
  - Squash-and-stretch flight physics.
- **🎨 5 Kitty Skins**:
  - **Orange Tabby** (Classic ginger striped kitty)
  - **Tuxedo Cat** (Dapper black & white with yellow eyes)
  - **Sweet Calico** (Patched tri-color kitty)
  - **Fluffy Marshmallow** (Pure cloud white with blue eyes)
  - **Midnight Void** (Mystical glossy black kitty with glowing eyes)
- **🔊 100% Procedural Web Audio API**:
  - Self-contained synthesized chirpy meows, bell score chimes, crunch snack effects, and collision bonks. No audio files to download or lose!
- **🌄 Dynamic Day-to-Night Skies**:
  - Parallax sky smoothly transitions from sunny daylight, to warm sunset, to starry night as your score rises.
- **🐟 Bonus Collectibles**:
  - Floating crispy fish biscuits and catnip treats spawn between posts for bonus points and sparkle bursts.
- **🏆 Medals & High Score**:
  - High score automatically saved to browser `localStorage`.
  - Rank awards: Bronze Bell 🥉, Silver Fish 🥈, Golden Collar 🥇, and Diamond Cat God 👑!
